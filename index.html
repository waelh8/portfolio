Case Studies

Senior judgement on landmark projects — where catching the right issue protected life safety, budget and handover.

HSBC Platinum — Data-Centre Life-Safety Catch

Life-safety
Challenge

During supervision of the bank's main data centre fit-out, the battery room lacked compliant hydrogen detection — a life-safety non-compliance that would fail authority handover.

Action

Flagged the gap, required rectification of the hydrogen-detection system and a full re-test against QCDD / NFPA requirements before sign-off.

Result

Non-compliance closed and the data centre handed over safely to authority — protecting occupants and the client's handover schedule.

🔌

PHCC Twin Towers — Transformer Overload Averted

Value engineering
Challenge

Site supervision revealed installed HVAC loads exceeded the owner's as-built figures, risking transformer overload — with no contractual route for the client to claim.

Action

Cut demand within transformer capacity: full LED conversion with daylight-sensor lighting control, fire-pump / HVAC interlock, and a dedicated CEO lift per tower.

Result

Load brought within existing transformer capacity — no new transformers, protecting the client from cost and the project from delay.

🏛️

Souq Waqif — Combined Underground Substation

Project management
Challenge

Three above-ground substations had to be removed from a sensitive heritage area without interrupting supply.

Action

As Project Manager, consolidated them into one combined 6×1.6 MVA underground substation with new outgoing feeders — coordinating directly with the Private & Royal Family Engineering Offices.

Result

Added capacity delivered underground and the streetscape freed — a politically sensitive project handled to the owner's satisfaction.
