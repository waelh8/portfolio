About

Senior electrical consultant engineer with approximately 21 years' experience across electrical and ELV design, design review, site supervision, testing and commissioning, MEP coordination and project close-out — delivered in Qatar, KSA and Egypt. I review and approve contractor design and submittals against KAHRAMAA, QCS, QCDD, IEC and NFPA requirements, resolve gaps between IFC design and site conditions, and lead the electrical technical position through to authority handover. Specialist experience in critical-power and data-center systems (UPS, LV distribution, earthing and ELV integration) on landmark Qatar projects including HSBC Platinum, the ISF data centers and high-rise fit-outs.
